What does “housing cost burden” mean?
“A household is said to be cost-burdened when it pays more than 30% of its income toward housing expenses and may have difficulty affording necessities such as food, clothing, transportation, and medical care.”
HUD FAQ, p. 8 · Published June 25, 2024

HUD also defines spending more than 50% of income on housing as severely cost burdened. HUD CHAS definitions

HUD compares total household housing costs, including utilities, with household income. This article compares advertised room rent with gross pay and optional aid examples, using HUD’s 30% benchmark as a comparison point. Loans add borrowed funds, not earnings. Values above 350% are shown numerically, but their markers stop at the chart’s edge. Methods and explanation
